Key Features Overview

Designed to operate securely, locally, and without exposing sensitive user contact details.

Privacy-First Architecture

Email addresses and phone numbers are completely masked. Connection requests are sent internally, and contact sharing is only enabled after explicit user consent.

Rule-Based Skill Recommendations

A local, rules-guided matching algorithm compares student skill requirements with the tech stack profiles of alumni to highlight perfect mentors automatically.

Verification Engine

Alumni validation is managed strictly by department administrators. Verification mechanisms ensure that only legitimate graduates enter the system.

Internship & Referral Pipeline

Alumni can directly post internal department referrals or mock job roles. Students are matching against these roles to fast-track career opportunities.

IT Department Vision

In the fast-evolving tech landscape, classrooms provide fundamental pillars, but real-world industry feedback makes graduates competent. AlumniLink bridges this transition gap securely.

Developed inside the IT Department, this server-hosted platform eliminates reliance on external SaaS applications, maintaining full sovereign ownership of data privacy and school network parameters.
